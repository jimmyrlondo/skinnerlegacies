Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Skinner Legacies LLC ("Skinner Legacies," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit skinnerlegacies.com, submit an inquiry through our website, or communicate with us by phone or text message.

This policy does not apply to protected health information created once you become a client of Skinner Legacies. That information is governed by our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, not this page.

Information We Collect

  • Information you submit through our website inquiry form: your name, email address, phone number, your relationship to Skinner Legacies (prospective client, caregiver, family member, referral partner, etc.), the service you're interested in, and any message you include.
  • Information from phone calls and text messages, if you contact us that way or if we contact you with your consent.

We do not currently use third-party analytics or advertising trackers on this website, and we do not use cookies for advertising purposes.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to respond to your inquiry, schedule and coordinate services, communicate with you about our services, and meet legal and regulatory recordkeeping requirements. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Text Messaging (SMS) Communications

Skinner Legacies may contact you by text message using the phone number you provide, for purposes such as responding to your inquiry, scheduling, appointment reminders, and general communication about our services. By submitting your phone number to us, including through our website inquiry form, you agree to the following:

  • Consent. You are consenting to receive text messages from Skinner Legacies. Consent is not a condition of receiving services, and providing your phone number is optional.
  • No sharing for marketing. Your personal information, including your phone number, will not be shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Message frequency. Message frequency varies depending on your interaction with us; messages are not sent on a fixed schedule.
  • Opt out anytime. Reply STOP to any message to opt out of future text messages at any time.
  • Get help. Reply HELP for more information, or contact us directly at 440-467-1555.
  • Rates. Message and data rates may apply.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Third-Party Services We Use

Our website and phone system rely on a small number of third-party services:

  • Formspree, to deliver inquiry form submissions to our email.
  • Google Maps, to display our location.
  • Google Fonts and Font Awesome, to load fonts and icons.
  • Ooma Office, our phone and text messaging provider.

These providers may process limited technical information (such as your IP address) as part of delivering their service. We don't control their privacy practices, so we encourage you to review their policies directly if you have questions.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. That said, no method of transmission over the internet, phone, or text message is completely secure. Please do not include highly sensitive medical information in our website contact form or in a text message. If you are already a client, please use the communication method your care team has provided for anything sensitive.

Data Retention

We retain inquiry and contact information for as long as needed to respond to your request, coordinate care, and meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website. Skinner Legacies does provide services to minors as part of our clinical programs; information collected in that context is handled under our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, not this policy.

Your Choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time as described above. You may also contact us to ask what contact information we have on file, or to request that it be deleted, to the extent we are not required to retain it by law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent version.
