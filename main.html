Services

Areas of support

These descriptions can be edited anytime as offerings and intake flow become more specific.

Diagnostic Assessment

A clinical evaluation of mental health, developmental, or substance use needs that results in a clear report and a recommended plan of care.

Outpatient Therapy

One-on-one and family counseling that addresses mental health, substance use, and the everyday stress that comes with them.

Case Management

Coordinated planning and advocacy that helps connect clients to providers, services, referrals, and supportive resources.

Crisis Intervention

Responsive care during urgent moments, including help identifying triggers ahead of time and building a plan to stay safe.

Psychosocial Rehabilitation

Skill-building support that encourages independence, confidence, and stronger social and emotional functioning.

Therapeutic Behavioral Services

Therapeutic care that supports emotional wellness, behavioral growth, and practical next steps for individuals and families.

Mental Health Day Treatment

Structured daytime support that encourages routine, engagement, therapeutic participation, and ongoing progress.

Substance Use Disorder Services

Assessment and treatment support for individuals navigating substance use, alongside the mental health care that often goes with it.

Grief Therapy

Compassionate support for individuals and families processing loss, at whatever pace that takes.

Telehealth Services

Secure video visits that bring therapy and support to wherever it's easiest to meet, no transportation or time off required.

OhioRISE Behavioral Health Respite

Short-term respite care for youth enrolled in OhioRISE, giving caregivers relief while maintaining steady, attentive support.
