Cost of Services

Understanding the cost of care before services are provided.

Skinner Legacies wants clients to understand potential costs before services are provided. Below is an overview of how billing works and our current self-pay rates. Your care team can always answer specific questions about your own coverage and expected costs.

Medicaid

Skinner Legacies bills Medicaid and applicable managed care organizations for covered medically necessary services. Medicaid clients will not be charged for covered services when prohibited by law. If a requested service is noncovered, not authorized, or may result in client responsibility, Skinner Legacies will discuss this before the service when applicable.

Commercial / Third-Party Coverage

When Skinner Legacies accepts a third-party payer, claims may be submitted on your behalf. You may be responsible for lawful deductibles, copayments, coinsurance, or noncovered amounts. Verification or authorization does not guarantee payment.

Good Faith Estimate

If you are uninsured or choose not to use insurance, you may have a federal right to receive a Good Faith Estimate of expected charges. Skinner Legacies will provide one when required. Estimates are based on information known at the time and may change if your needs or services change.

Please notify Skinner Legacies of changes in coverage or payment information. You have the right to ask questions about fees and known financial responsibility. See our complete Good Faith Estimate and Financial Policy for how estimates are prepared, delivered, and handled.
